What to Include in Your Message

The more detail you give, the faster we can help. When you write, please include:

  • For a broken link: the exact video link you tried, the platform it is from (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and so on), and what happened — did it show an error, or did nothing load at all?
  • Your device and browser: for example "Android phone, Chrome" or "iPhone, Safari" or "Windows, Edge". Many problems are specific to one device.
  • For a new site request: the name and address of the site you want us to support.
  • For feedback: what you like, what is slow, and what is missing. We build the site around what people actually ask for.

Before You Write — Quick Fixes

Most problems have a quick fix. Try these first:

  • Try a public video. Private, deleted, or age-restricted videos cannot be downloaded. Check that the video plays in your browser while logged out.
  • Copy the link again. Use the video's own Share button or address bar. A shortened or partial link may fail.
  • Wait a few minutes and retry. TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook sometimes block server-side downloads temporarily; the same link often works a little later.
  • Try a different browser or device. If it works on one device but not another, the problem is usually on that device.
  • Check your connection. A slow or interrupted connection can stop a download halfway.

If none of that helps, write to us with the details above and we will investigate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my link fail?

Private, deleted, or age-restricted videos cannot be downloaded. Some platforms also block server-side downloads temporarily. Try a public video link, copy the link again from the video page, and retry after a few minutes.

Can I download videos in 1080p or 4K?

FastVideoDown shows every quality the video was published in. Some platforms only offer high qualities to their own apps, so the highest available quality may be 360p or 720p for some videos.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no sign-up, and no app to install. Paste a link and download.

Is FastVideoDown free?

Yes — free forever, with no daily limit and no watermark on the videos you save.

Can you add my favourite site?

Probably! Email us the site's name and address and we will look into it. Popular requests are built first.
