How to Download YouTube Videos — The Easy Way

Want to save a YouTube video to watch offline? You don't need an app, an extension or an account. With FastVideoDown, a free online YouTube video downloader, you can save any public YouTube video in 360p, 720p, 1080p or 4K — or save just the audio as MP3. Here's how, step by step.

Step 1: Copy the YouTube link

Open YouTube and find the video you want. Tap Share under the video, then Copy link. On a computer, just copy the URL from the address bar.

Step 2: Paste it into FastVideoDown

Open FastVideoDown, paste the link into the downloader box and click Get Video. In a few seconds you'll see the video's title, thumbnail, duration and every available quality.

Step 3: Pick a quality and download

Choose the quality you want — 360p for a small file, 720p or 1080p HD for everyday watching, 4K for the sharpest picture. Click Download and the file saves straight from YouTube's own high-speed servers.

How to download YouTube to MP3

Only need the sound? After pasting the link, look for the Audio only section and click Download. The audio saves in its original format, which plays on any phone or computer just like an MP3. Perfect for music, podcasts, lectures and speeches.

Why 1080p sometimes has no sound

YouTube serves high qualities (1080p and 4K) as video-only streams, with the audio separate. If you pick 1080p and get no sound, choose the highest option under Video with sound instead — that's usually 720p with audio included.
