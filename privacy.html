Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026.

The short version: FastVideoDown does not collect, store, or sell any personal information. There is no account to create, no email to give, and no history kept. A link you paste is processed on the fly and forgotten immediately. We set no cookies and run no analytics ourselves. The site is free because it shows ads: our advertising partner (Adsterra) may set cookies and collect limited technical information to show and measure those ads — see section 5 below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. FastVideoDown does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profile. You can use every feature of the site without telling us who you are.

We do not keep a database of users, and we do not build profiles of visitors. There is nothing to leak, because there is nothing stored.

2. What Happens When You Paste a Link

When you paste a video link into FastVideoDown, our server reads the link, asks the video platform for the available download options, and shows them to you. That is the whole process:

  • The link is used once, for that one request, and then discarded. It is never saved to a database, a file, or a log.
  • The video file itself is never stored on our servers. Downloads come straight from the video platform's own servers to your device.
  • We do not keep a history of the links you pasted, the videos you looked up, or the files you downloaded.
  • Nothing you paste is shared with anyone else.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

FastVideoDown itself does not set cookies. We do not set tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies of our own, and we do not use your browser's local storage — there is no "recent downloads" list saved on your device, because the site keeps no record of what you download.

Our advertising partner, however, may set cookies or similar technologies in your browser to deliver and measure ads (for example, to count how often an ad is shown, or to limit how often you see the same ad). Those cookies belong to the advertising partner, not to FastVideoDown, and are governed by that partner's own privacy policy — see section 5. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the downloader itself will still work, but some ads may not display correctly.

4. Analytics and Tracking

We do not run Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other analytics or tracking service on FastVideoDown. We do not count individual visitors, follow you across the web, or measure what you do on the site.

The only counts we ever see are the anonymous, aggregate numbers our hosting provider reports (for example, total page loads), which cannot identify any person. Advertising measurement (for example, how many times an ad was shown) is handled by our advertising partner, as described in section 5, and we do not receive personal information from it.

5. Advertising — How This Free Service Is Paid For

FastVideoDown is free, with no account and no subscription. It is paid for by advertising, served by our advertising partner Adsterra. Ads appear as banners on the site, as a small notification-style Social Bar, as an ad shown while a video is being found, and as an advertising page that may open in a new tab when you click a Download button. Clicking a Download button always downloads your video as well — the ad never replaces the download.

To serve and measure ads, Adsterra may collect limited technical information, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location (country or city, from your IP address), and which ads were shown or clicked. Adsterra may set cookies for this purpose, as described in section 3. Adsterra does not receive your name, email address, or the video links you paste from us, because we never collect those things — but Adsterra's own privacy policy applies to the information it collects directly, and you can read it on Adsterra's website.

We do not control the content of individual ads, and an ad does not mean we endorse the product or website it promotes. If an ad looks misleading or unsafe, please report it to us through the Contact page and we will pass it on to the advertising partner.

Ad blockers: the site asks you to turn off your ad blocker before using it, because ads are what keep the service free. If an ad blocker (including a browser with built-in blocking, such as Brave) is active, you will see a notice asking you to disable it and reload the page. We do not punish or track users for using an ad blocker — the site simply cannot be used while one is active.

6. Third-Party Services

Two kinds of third parties are involved when you use FastVideoDown, and neither receives your personal information from us:

  • The video platform (for example YouTube, TikTok, or Facebook). When you download a video, your browser connects to that platform's servers directly to fetch the file, exactly as if you were watching the video there. That platform's own privacy policy applies to that connection.
  • Our hosting providers (Cloudflare Pages for the website and our API server). They deliver the pages and process download requests. They do not receive your name, email, or any account information from us, because we never collect it.
  • Our advertising partner (Adsterra). It serves the ads on the site and may set cookies and collect limited technical information for advertising, as described in section 5. It does not receive personal information from us, because we never collect any.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers. We hold no personal information to share; the only information the advertising partner receives is the technical information it collects itself when serving an ad, as described in section 5.

7. Data Security

Because we store no personal data, there is no personal data to protect — and no personal data that could be exposed in a breach. The site is served over HTTPS, so the connection between your browser and FastVideoDown is encrypted. Download requests are processed in memory and discarded; no video files or links are written to disk.

8. Children's Privacy

FastVideoDown is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since we collect no information from anyone, there is nothing to delete — but if you are a parent and have a concern, please contact us and we will help.

9. Your Rights

Under privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe) and the CCPA (California), you have rights over your personal data — including the right to see it, correct it, or delete it. With FastVideoDown, these rights are simple to honour: we hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, or erase. If you ever believe we hold something about you, write to us and we will investigate and confirm in writing.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the service changes. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of FastVideoDown after a change means you accept the updated policy. If we ever start collecting information — which we do not plan to do — we will say so here clearly before it happens.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your privacy on FastVideoDown? See our Contact page or write to vividsaiful.contact@gmail.com.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use FastVideoDown?

No. There is no account, no sign-up, and no login. Every feature works without giving us any information about you.

Do you track what I download?

No. Links are processed on the fly and forgotten immediately. We keep no download history, on our servers or in your browser.

Do you use cookies?

FastVideoDown itself sets no cookies of any kind. Our advertising partner (Adsterra) may set cookies to show and measure ads — see section 5. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the downloader will still work.

Why does the site ask me to turn off my ad blocker?

FastVideoDown is free because it shows ads. If an ad blocker is active — including a browser with built-in blocking such as Brave — the site shows a notice asking you to disable it and reload. The downloader itself is still free, with no account and no payment.

Does clicking Download open an ad?

Sometimes, yes. When you click a Download button, your video downloads and an advertising page may also open in a new tab. The ad never replaces or delays your download.

Is my email safe if I write to you?

Yes. If you email us, we only use your address to reply to you. We never share it, sell it, or add it to a mailing list.

Can I ask you to delete my data?

Of course — but there is nothing to delete, because we store no personal data. Write to us and we will confirm that in writing.
