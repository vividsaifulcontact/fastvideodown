Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026.

The short version: FastVideoDown does not collect, store, or sell any personal information. There is no account to create, no email to give, and no history kept. A link you paste is processed on the fly and forgotten immediately. We do not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

1. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. FastVideoDown does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profile. You can use every feature of the site without telling us who you are.

We do not keep a database of users, and we do not build profiles of visitors. There is nothing to leak, because there is nothing stored.

2. What Happens When You Paste a Link

When you paste a video link into FastVideoDown, our server reads the link, asks the video platform for the available download options, and shows them to you. That is the whole process:

  • The link is used once, for that one request, and then discarded. It is never saved to a database, a file, or a log.
  • The video file itself is never stored on our servers. Downloads come straight from the video platform's own servers to your device.
  • We do not keep a history of the links you pasted, the videos you looked up, or the files you downloaded.
  • Nothing you paste is shared with anyone else.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

FastVideoDown does not use cookies. We do not set tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies in your browser.

We do not use your browser's local storage either. There is no "recent downloads" list saved on your device, because the site keeps no record of what you download. Clearing your browser data changes nothing, because there is nothing of ours to clear.

4. Analytics and Tracking

We do not run Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other analytics or tracking service on FastVideoDown. We do not count individual visitors, follow you across the web, or measure what you do on the site.

The only counts we ever see are the anonymous, aggregate numbers our hosting provider reports (for example, total page loads), which cannot identify any person.

5. Third-Party Services

Two kinds of third parties are involved when you use FastVideoDown, and neither receives your personal information from us:

  • The video platform (for example YouTube, TikTok, or Facebook). When you download a video, your browser connects to that platform's servers directly to fetch the file, exactly as if you were watching the video there. That platform's own privacy policy applies to that connection.
  • Our hosting providers (Cloudflare Pages for the website and our API server). They deliver the pages and process download requests. They do not receive your name, email, or any account information from us, because we never collect it.

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with advertisers or data brokers, because there is no information to share.

6. Data Security

Because we store no personal data, there is no personal data to protect — and no personal data that could be exposed in a breach. The site is served over HTTPS, so the connection between your browser and FastVideoDown is encrypted. Download requests are processed in memory and discarded; no video files or links are written to disk.

7. Children's Privacy

FastVideoDown is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since we collect no information from anyone, there is nothing to delete — but if you are a parent and have a concern, please contact us and we will help.

8. Your Rights

Under privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe) and the CCPA (California), you have rights over your personal data — including the right to see it, correct it, or delete it. With FastVideoDown, these rights are simple to honour: we hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, or erase. If you ever believe we hold something about you, write to us and we will investigate and confirm in writing.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the service changes. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of FastVideoDown after a change means you accept the updated policy. If we ever start collecting information — which we do not plan to do — we will say so here clearly before it happens.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your privacy on FastVideoDown? See our Contact page or write to contact@fastvideodown.com.

No. FastVideoDown sets no cookies of any kind — no tracking, no analytics, no advertising cookies.
