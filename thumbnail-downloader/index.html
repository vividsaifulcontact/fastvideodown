What is a video thumbnail?

A thumbnail is the cover image shown before a video plays. Creators, designers and marketers often need that image for a blog post, a presentation, a social-media post or a video of their own. A thumbnail downloader saves that image as a file (JPG or PNG) so you can use it wherever you need it. Download only thumbnails you own or have permission to use.
